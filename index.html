Surveillance Awareness

Drive Free. Drive Informed.
Avoid ALPR Cameras.

A premium privacy-preserving utility for iOS. FlockSpot checks real-time location metrics, maps Automatic License Plate Reader networks, and customizes routes to maintain privacy.

🛡️

ALPR Camera Avoidance

Calculates routes that actively avoid Automatic License Plate Reader networks. Uses Valhalla routing endpoints with dynamically generated exclusion zones.

🧭

Target Compass

Shows a persistent compass overlay pointing exactly toward the nearest surveillance scanner, maintaining a live indicator of nearby infrastructure.

🚗

CarPlay Integration

Includes a dedicated template for vehicle CarPlay displays, providing active alert interfaces and a listing of the closest camera points.

🔊

Voice Navigation

Features step-by-step turn guidance and system voice synthesizers to announce avoidance updates and route changes.
